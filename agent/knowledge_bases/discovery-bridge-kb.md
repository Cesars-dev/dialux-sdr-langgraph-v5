# Discovery Bridge KB — From Prospect's Words to Phone Frame

> Retrieved when: bridging the prospect's {{interest_topic}} and {{pain_frame}} to what happens when calls go unanswered.
> KB name: discovery-bridge-kb

## How to use this KB
The prospect gave you their {{interest_topic}} and {{pain_frame}} in their exact words. This KB gives you the ANGLE to bridge their words to the phone-coverage frame. Generate your question from their words + the angle below — never read a question verbatim.

Phrases in < > are examples to vary, not copy verbatim.

Ask ONE question at a time. Go 2-3 questions deep on the angle until the prospect acknowledges the phone gap. Capture the acknowledgment the moment you hear it — set {{pattern_matched}} = true when they connect their {{pain_frame}} to what happens when calls go unanswered.

---

## IF {{interest_topic}} = MISSED CALLS (or similar words)

### Angle 1: Who answers
<When a call comes in and you're out on a job, who's picking it up?>
<Who handles the calls when you're in the field?>
<Is there someone at the desk full-time, or does it depend on the day?>

### Angle 2: Volume
<How many would you say you miss a day?>
<On a busy day, what's that number look like?>
<Roughly how many calls a week hit voicemail?>

### Angle 3: What happens to the missed call
<Those calls that go to voicemail — do they leave a message, or do they just call the next guy?>
<Do you know what happens after it rings out?>
<Do they usually call you back?>

### Angle 4: After-hours
<What happens after you close? Who's answering then?>
<What about evenings and weekends?>
<After-hours calls — where do they go?>

---

## IF {{interest_topic}} = REVENUE / MONEY / LOSING MONEY

### Angle 1: Where the money walks
<When a call goes unanswered, what do you think happens to that revenue?>
<If you don't pick up, does that customer come back?>
<What's the cost of one missed call to you?>

### Angle 2: Volume bridge
<Do you know roughly how many calls a week go unanswered?>
<How many of those would have been revenue if you'd picked up?>

### Angle 3: The uncertainty
<Of those missed calls — do you know which ones were the big jobs versus the small ones?>
<You don't know which missed call was the $3,500 job, right?>
<They all just look like voicemails?>

---

## IF {{interest_topic}} = NEED MORE LEADS

### Angle 1: Where leads come from
<When you get a lead, how does it usually come in — phone, email, web form?>

### Angle 2: What happens when leads call
<When a lead calls in and no one picks up, what happens to that lead?>
<If they don't reach you the first time, do they try again or move on?>

### Angle 3: Cost per lead
<You paid to acquire that lead — what happens to that money when the call goes to voicemail?>
<What's it cost you to get a lead, and how many of those calls go unanswered?>

---

## IF {{interest_topic}} = RECEPTIONIST / STAFF / TURNOVER

### Angle 1: Coverage gaps
<When she's at lunch or on the other line, who's answering?>
<What happens when she steps away?>
<Who covers her breaks?>

### Angle 2: After she leaves
<When she quits or is out, how long until you have coverage again?>
<What happened the last time you didn't have someone at the desk?>

### Angle 3: The real cost
<What's a receptionist cost you fully loaded — and how many calls does she actually catch in a week?>
<How many calls does she miss in a week even when she's there?>

---

## IF {{interest_topic}} = CUSTOMER SERVICE / COMPLAINTS

### Angle 1: Hold times
<When customers call and get put on hold, what happens?>
<How long are they willing to wait before they hang up?>

### Angle 2: After-hours
<When customers call after you close, what's their experience?>
<Where do those calls go?>

### Angle 3: The complaint that walks
<When a frustrated customer can't reach you, what do they do?>
<Do they call back, or do they call someone else?>

---

## IF {{interest_topic}} = AFTER-HOURS / WEEKENDS

### Angle 1: Volume
<How many calls come in after hours in a week?>

### Angle 2: What happens
<Those after-hours calls — where do they go?>
<Who's picking up at 9 PM?>

### Angle 3: The competitor
<If they don't reach you at 9 PM, who do they call?>

---

## IF {{interest_topic}} = SCALING / GROWTH

### Angle 1: The bottleneck
<As you grow, where's the first thing that breaks?>
<If you doubled your calls next month, what would break first?>

### Angle 2: Volume projection
<If you doubled your calls next month, could you handle them?>
<How many more calls can your current setup absorb?>

### Angle 3: The cost of growth
<What does it cost you to add another body to answer phones?>
<How long does it take to hire and train someone for the phones?>

---

## IF {{interest_topic}} = NO-SHOWS / APPOINTMENTS

### Angle 1: The booking gap
<When a customer calls to book and no one answers, what happens to that appointment?>
<Do they call back, or book with someone else?>

### Angle 2: Reminder gap
<How are you reminding customers of appointments now?>
<What happens when a reminder call goes to voicemail?>

### Angle 3: The cost
<What's a no-show cost you — and how many of those start with an unanswered call?>

---

## IF {{interest_topic}} DOESN'T MATCH ANY ABOVE

Use the closest angle and adapt. The pattern is always:

1. Connect their {{interest_topic}} to what happens when a call comes in
2. Ask about who answers / what happens when they don't
3. Ask about volume
4. Ask about what happens to the missed call (competitor, lost revenue, etc.)

Go 2-3 questions deep until they acknowledge the phone gap.

---

## When to set {{pattern_matched}} = true

The prospect has matched their {{pain_frame}} to the phone solution when they acknowledge any of the following:

- Calls go to voicemail (and that's a problem)
- Missed callers call the competitor
- Revenue walks out the door when calls go unanswered
- Leads are lost when no one picks up
- After-hours calls go nowhere
- The receptionist gap is where calls die

When you hear this acknowledgment — capture it immediately: set {{pattern_matched}} = true.
