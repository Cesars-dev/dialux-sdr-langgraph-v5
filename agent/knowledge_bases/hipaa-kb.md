# HIPAA & Data Handling Knowledge Base

## The golden phrasing rule

NEVER say "we are HIPAA compliant" as a badge claim. ALWAYS answer the question
"**how do we handle HIPAA compliance?**" — describe the PROCESS. Anchor on
industry standards. This sounds more credible and it is exactly what we do.

## How Dialux handles HIPAA compliance (the true process)

### 1. BAA signed BEFORE any PHI is touched
- Dialux signs a **Business Associate Agreement with the practice on day one — before we ever touch a single piece of patient information.**
- Say it like: <First thing — before we touch any patient data, we sign a BAA. That's the legal contract that puts us under HIPAA obligations.>

### 2. Two-phase onboarding — Dialux never sees PHI
- **Phase 1 — "stop the bleeding":** we prepare the agent, lock in the calendar, and set everything up. Then we hand over: **the client re-writes the passwords and from that moment we no longer have access.** Dialux never sees patient data.
- **Phase 2 — the cyber-security bunker:** the production environment is hardened to full industry-standard security controls.
- Say it like: <We set everything up, then hand you the keys — you change the passwords, and after that we have no access to your patient data at all. On top of that, the production setup runs in a fully hardened security environment.>

### 3. Industry-standard controls (speak in standards, not in certifications)
- **Encryption in transit and at rest** on everything that touches the system.
- **Least-privilege access:** only what each role needs; credentials rotated at handoff.
- **Auditability:** every access and action is logged.

### 4. Why healthcare practices pay more (internal context — do NOT quote pricing)
Medical/dental deployments carry the compliance overhead (BAA, phased handoff,
bunker environment). This is WHY the medical tier is priced differently. Pricing
itself is NEVER quoted here — defer to the live call per the pricing rule.
