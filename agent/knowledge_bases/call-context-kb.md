# Call Context & Intent Knowledge Base

> Retrieved when: the prospect references an outreach channel (how they heard about Dialux), or why they're calling.
> KB name: call-context-kb

---

## Voicemail Callback

When they're returning a voicemail you (Dialux) left:
- Open warm and acknowledge the callback: <Oh hey, thanks so much for calling me back!>
- Reference the message lightly: <I left you a note about stopping missed calls — what made you call back today?>
- Treat as a warm inbound — do NOT re-pitch cold. Mirror their reason, then run discovery.
- Don't apologize for the outreach or sound salesy.

---

## Indeed Job Post

When they reference an Indeed job posting (Dialux hiring):
- Clarify intent first — job posts attract candidates AND business owners:
  <Just to point you to the right place — are you calling about the job posting, or about phone coverage for a business?>
- If a job seeker: take a name + number + role of interest politely, tell them the team will follow up, then end the call. Do NOT run the sales flow.
- If a business owner who saw the post: <Great — then you're in the right place.> → run discovery.

---

## Facebook / Meta Ad

When they clicked a Facebook or Meta ad:
- <Great, glad it caught your eye!>
- The ad hook is missed-call revenue. Confirm the pain: <You mentioned missed calls — tell me what's been happening with yours.>
- Lean straight into the missed-revenue angle; they're already warm to the outcome.

---

## Cold Outreach (We Reached Them)

When they're calling back after a cold call / outbound from the Dialux team:
- <Appreciate you getting back to me.>
- Do NOT assume interest. Discover their situation first: <What made you reach out today?>
- Mirror, then run discovery.

---

## General Inbound (Default)

For any other inbound call with no clear channel:
- Greet, then ask what prompted the call: <What made you reach out today?>
- Mirror their reason, show empathy, build rapport (2–3 questions), then run discovery.
- If you can't tell the channel, don't force one — just discover.
